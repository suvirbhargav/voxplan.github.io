Calendar access

With your permission, VoxPlan uses Apple's calendar access (EventKit) to show your calendars and to create, change or delete events when you ask it to.

Your events only leave your phone in the request described above: the title, time and location of upcoming events, so Mistral AI can understand which event you mean. They aren't stored, sold or used for anything else.

No ads, no tracking

VoxPlan has no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites, build a profile of you, or sell your data. Your data is used only to make the app work.

Who handles your data

  • Cloudflare runs our small server (a Cloudflare Worker). It passes your request on and keeps the free-limit counter.
  • Mistral AI processes requests in the EU. It turns your voice into text and your request into calendar changes. Requests are processed and then discarded.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, so we can find and fix crashes.

Keeping and deleting

Our server keeps no recordings, requests or events after it has answered. The only thing it keeps is the usage counter for your random app ID.

To remove what VoxPlan has stored on your phone, open Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.

Your rights

If you're in the EU or EEA, you can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Because VoxPlan keeps almost everything on your phone, there's usually nothing on our side but an anonymous counter.

Children

VoxPlan isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from them.
