Voice calendar for iPhone

Say it, and it's in your calendar.

VoxPlan turns what you say or type into calendar events, with a little pixel fox to keep you company.

What it does

  • Speak or typeSay or type “lunch with Anna tomorrow at 12” and VoxPlan creates the event. Title, date, time and place are filled in for you.
  • Change things the same way“Move lunch to 1” or “cancel the gym tonight”. Changes and deletions always ask you first.
  • A pixel fox that lives with youYour fox walks on the app's buttons, reacts to your day, and grows closer the more you tap it.
  • In your Dynamic IslandWhen a reminder is due, your fox pops up in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen with the event.
  • WidgetsToday's events, a monthly calendar you can flip through right on your Home Screen, and the fox.
  • Your calendar, your phoneWorks with the calendars already on your iPhone. In 15 languages, with no account to create.

Privacy

Your calendar stays on your iPhone. When you speak or type a request, it's sent with your next few events to Mistral AI in the EU, understood, and then discarded. No ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

The privacy policy lists exactly what leaves your phone and why.

Free to use

VoxPlan is free. Voice requests have a limit of 2 a day and 5 a week; typed requests don't count toward it.
